<section class="home-intro">
  <p class="section-kicker">Social recommendation / Causal reasoning / Graph learning</p>
  <h1>Building recommender systems that understand social behavior, not only social links.</h1>
  <p class="home-lead">I study how people form preferences, friendships, and social influence patterns in online platforms. My recent work combines graph neural networks, causal inference, dynamic graph learning, and large language models to make recommender systems more accurate, more interpretable, and more faithful to real social mechanisms.</p>

<section class="home-section">
  <h2>Research Focus</h2>
  <div class="focus-grid">
    <div class="focus-item">
      <h3>Social Recommendation</h3>
      <p>Modeling when social ties truly help recommendation, when they introduce noise, and how recommender systems can distinguish preference similarity from social influence.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="focus-item">
      <h3>Causal Reasoning</h3>
      <p>Using counterfactual thinking to refine social graphs, estimate friend influence, and avoid treating every observed social link as equally useful evidence.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="focus-item">
      <h3>Graph and LLM Methods</h3>
      <p>Designing graph learning models that combine structural signals with textual, personality, temporal, and diffusion features from real social platforms.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

    <article>
      <span>HICSS 2025</span>
      <h3>LLM-enhanced friend recommendation through personality disentanglement</h3>
